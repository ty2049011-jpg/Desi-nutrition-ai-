Indian Diet for Weight Loss

Fat loss comes from eating a little less energy than you use. A deficit of about 500 kcal below maintenance is a common starting point and is estimated to lower weight by roughly 0.45 kg per week. Keep protein high so you stay full and keep muscle.

Step 1: find your calorie target

Use the calorie calculator. For a 30-year-old man (70 kg, 175 cm, moderately active) it gives about 2,060 kcal per day.

Step 2: set protein

A planning value is about 2.0 g per kg of body weight, so 70 kg means about 140 g. Check yours in the protein calculator.

Step 3: build the plate

Half the plate sabzi and salad, a quarter dal, sprouts, paneer, egg or chicken, a quarter roti or rice, plus curd. Measure rice and oil, because they add calories quickly.

Frequently asked questions

How many rotis can I eat for weight loss?

It depends on your calorie target. A roti is roughly 120 kcal, so 2 to 3 per meal fits many plans. Count the rest of the meal too.

Is rice allowed?

Yes. Portion matters more than the food itself: one cup of cooked rice is roughly 200 kcal.

How fast should I lose weight?

A gradual pace is easier to sustain. Treat 0.45 kg per week as an estimate, not a promise.
